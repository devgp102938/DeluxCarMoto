ABOUT LAMBORGHINI

Lamborghini is an Italian manufacturer of luxury super sports cars, founded in 1963 by Ferruccio Lamborghini, and is currently based in Sant'Agata Bolognese, Italy. The company was created to compete with other luxury car brands like Ferrari and, is known for high-performance vehicles like the Countach, Diablo, and the first V12 super sports car, the 350 GT. Today, it produces a range of high-performance electrified vehicles, including the Revuelto, the Urus SE.
