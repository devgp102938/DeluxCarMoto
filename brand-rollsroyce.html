ABOUT Rolls-Royce

Rolls-Royce is a British company with a rich history of both luxury automobiles and high-performance engines for aerospace and defense. It was founded in 1904 by the partnership of Charles Rolls and Henry Royce, who aimed to build "the Best Car in the World". Today, Rolls-Royce Motor Cars is a subsidiary of BMW that produces high-end luxury cars at its Goodwood, England headquarters, while the separate company, Rolls-Royce Holdings plc, is a global leader in power systems for aviation and other industries.
