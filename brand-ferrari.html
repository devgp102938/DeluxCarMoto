ABOUT FERRARI

Ferrari is an Italian luxury sports car manufacturer founded by Enzo Ferrari in 1939, known for high-performance vehicles and its successful history in Formula 1 racing. The company, with its iconic "Prancing Horse" logo, produces a range of supercars and grand tourers and is based in Maranello, Italy. Ferrari originally focused on racing, with road cars developed to finance the racing division, and has been a public company since 1960.
