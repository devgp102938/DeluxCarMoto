ABOUT LAND ROVER

Land Rover is a British brand of four-wheel-drive, off-road capable vehicles, now owned by the Indian company Tata Motors and part of Jaguar Land Rover (JLR). The first Land Rover, designed by Maurice Wilks in 1947, debuted in 1948, based on a Jeep chassis and axle, and was initially a utilitarian vehicle. The brand has since evolved to produce luxury SUVs, with iconic models like the Range Rover (introduced in 1970) and the Discovery.
